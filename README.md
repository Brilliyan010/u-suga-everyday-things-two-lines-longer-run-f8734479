# Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run

An escalator, explained to a 10-year-old in two lines: a staircase that learned to walk.

## Entry

> An escalator is a staircase that learned to walk, carrying you upstairs while your feet just stand and watch.
>
> It maps steps rising one after another beneath you, but leaves out that real stairs never move and never get tired the way your legs do.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22.18+) writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` and
self-checks the gate first: 1–400 characters, exactly two lines, one sentence
each. The text is fixed, so every run is byte-identical.
